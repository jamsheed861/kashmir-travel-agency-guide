How to Choose a Travel Agency in Kashmir: 10 Things to Check Before Booking

By Travel My Kashmir Editorial Team • Last Updated: Nov 3, 2026

Planning a holiday in Kashmir often means coordinating several services: airport transfers, sightseeing, hotels, houseboats, local activities and travel between destinations such as Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg. A suitable travel agency in Kashmir can bring these arrangements together, but choosing one should involve more than comparing a package price or a star rating.

The right agency for your trip is one that understands your itinerary, explains what is included, uses dependable local suppliers, gives you written booking terms and remains reachable when plans need to change. This guide explains what to check before paying an advance.

1. Confirm the agency's identity and local presence

Start by confirming that the business is real and that you know which company is responsible for your booking. Check its trading name, office address, working phone number, website and the name of the person or team handling your enquiry. Search for the same business details across its website, business profiles and invoices. Inconsistencies are a reason to ask questions before proceeding.

Ask the agency for the applicable Jammu and Kashmir tourism or travel-trade registration details, where relevant, and verify those details with the appropriate authority. Do not rely only on a logo, badge or a statement such as “government approved”. Ask what the registration covers and whether the name on the document matches the business taking your payment.

There is an important distinction between local registration and recognition by India's Ministry of Tourism. The Ministry's recognition scheme for tourism service providers is voluntary; absence from that particular national recognition list should not, by itself, be treated as proof that an agency is illegitimate. Check the registration or permissions that apply to the business and services being offered. See the Ministry of Tourism's information on recognised tourism service providers.

2. Read recent reviews, not just the overall rating

Reviews can help you understand how an agency behaves after a booking is made. Read recent, detailed feedback on more than one independent platform where possible. Look for comments about communication, punctuality, vehicle condition, hotel accuracy, problem-solving and whether the services delivered matched the written itinerary.

A large number of short, identical or unusually generic reviews tells you less than a smaller number of detailed accounts. Check how the agency responds to complaints. A professional response should address the issue rather than attack the reviewer. Reviews are useful evidence, but they are not a substitute for a written quotation and clear booking terms.

3. Ask for an itinerary that fits your actual trip

A good Kashmir itinerary should be built around your arrival and departure times, trip length, group size, interests, mobility needs and preferred pace. Ask the agency to show the destinations planned for each day and identify where you will stay each night.

For example, a 5-night trip might include time in Srinagar, a day in Gulmarg and a stay or excursion in Pahalgam. That is only a sample structure: the best order depends on the season, road conditions, hotel availability and your flight schedule. Avoid an itinerary that tries to cover too many distant places in a short period without explaining the travel involved.

Before confirming, ask:

  • Which destinations and sightseeing stops are included each day?
  • How many nights are planned in each place?
  • Are transfers private or shared?
  • Are sightseeing visits flexible, or are fixed timings involved?
  • What happens if a road, attraction or activity is unavailable because of weather or local restrictions?
  • Can the itinerary be adjusted for children, older travellers or people who prefer less walking?

A reliable planner should explain the practical limits of the itinerary instead of promising that every attraction will always be accessible.

4. Compare the full quotation, not only the headline price

Ask for an itemised written quotation before paying. It should identify the travel dates, number of guests, room configuration, vehicle category, meal plan, destinations, number of nights, inclusions, exclusions, taxes and payment schedule.

Check whether the quote includes or excludes items such as tolls, parking, local union-operated transfers, pony rides, activity tickets, guide charges, meals beyond the stated plan and optional sightseeing. Which items apply can vary by route and activity, so ask the agency to specify them for your own itinerary.

Be cautious of a very low quote that does not name the hotels or vehicle category. Ask whether the price can change, what would cause a change and whether any substitution requires your approval. A clear quote makes it easier to compare agencies fairly.

5. Check the vehicle and driver arrangements

If your package includes private transport, confirm the vehicle category and seating capacity in writing. A sedan, SUV, premium SUV and Tempo Traveller are not interchangeable: passenger capacity, luggage space and comfort can differ considerably. Tell the agency how many adults and children are travelling and how many bags you expect to carry.

Ask whether the vehicle is assigned for the full itinerary or whether different vehicles or local transfers may be used on particular routes. Confirm the arrangements for airport pickup, driver contact, waiting time, night travel, parking and any additional charges. If a specific model matters to you, ask whether it is guaranteed or only an example of the category.

You should also ask how drivers are selected and briefed, whether the vehicle is commercially authorised and maintained for the service, and whom to contact if a vehicle or driver issue arises. Local route knowledge is valuable, but the agency should not encourage unsafe driving or unrealistic travel times.

6. Verify hotel details before confirming the package

The words “deluxe”, “premium” or “luxury” do not describe a consistent standard across every property. Ask for the exact hotel name, location, room category, meal plan and number of nights. If the agency offers alternatives, request the names and differences before accepting a substitution.

Useful questions include:

  • Is the quoted room category the one that will be booked?
  • Is the hotel close to the area or attraction described in the itinerary?
  • Are heating, hot water, Wi-Fi, parking and meal services available during your dates?
  • Are children accommodated in the quoted room, and are extra-bed charges applicable?
  • What are the check-in, check-out and cancellation conditions?
  • Can the agency provide current photographs or the property's official listing?

If hotel quality is important to you, ask how the agency evaluates the properties it recommends. Does it rely on recent guest feedback, direct supplier checks, staff visits or a combination? Do not assume that every agency personally inspects every room. Ask what the process actually is and get the confirmed property in writing.

7. Get exact details for houseboats and Shikara rides

A houseboat stay is not the same as a hotel stay, and facilities vary from one property to another. Before paying, ask for the houseboat's name, lake area, room category, bathroom arrangement, heating availability for your travel dates, meal plan, transfer arrangements and cancellation terms. Confirm whether a Shikara transfer or sightseeing ride is included, and whether it is separate from the houseboat booking.

For a Shikara ride on Dal Lake, agree on the expected duration, route, number of passengers, price, waiting time and any additional charges before starting. Ask who is providing the ride and how to contact the agency if the service does not match the booking. Do not assume that a ride is included simply because a package mentions a houseboat stay.

8. Understand activity tickets and local arrangements

Popular activities such as the Gulmarg Gondola, skiing, pony rides, rafting and guided walks may depend on weather, operating schedules, local rules, availability and separate suppliers. Ask whether the agency is arranging a ticket, making a booking enquiry or merely including the activity as an optional suggestion.

For any activity, clarify the provider, date, ticket category, inclusions, refund or rescheduling terms and safety arrangements. Do not treat an activity as confirmed until you have the relevant booking confirmation or ticket. Where official online booking is available, check the current rules and availability through the authorised channel.

9. Read payment, cancellation and change policies

Before transferring money, ask for an invoice or booking confirmation that identifies the business, services, dates, total cost, amount paid and balance due. Use a traceable payment method and keep receipts. Confirm the account or payment recipient with the agency through a contact channel you have independently checked.

Read the cancellation and amendment policy carefully. It should explain what happens if you cancel, change your dates, arrive late, miss a service or if a hotel or activity becomes unavailable. Ask whether hotel, transport and activity suppliers have separate cancellation conditions. Do not rely on a verbal promise that “everything is refundable”.

10. Check who supports you during the trip

A travel agency's responsibility should not end when it sends a voucher. Before booking, find out who you can contact during the trip, during what hours, and how urgent issues are handled. Ask for an on-trip contact and a backup number. Clarify who coordinates a replacement vehicle, hotel issue or itinerary change if the original arrangement cannot be delivered.

For a multi-day Kashmir holiday, this support can matter as much as the initial price. Weather, road conditions, traffic, operating schedules and supplier availability can change. A responsible agency should communicate changes clearly and explain the available alternatives.

A simple checklist for comparing Kashmir travel agencies

Use the same questions for every agency you contact. This makes the comparison fairer and helps you avoid choosing on price alone.

What to compare What a clear answer should include
Business identity Legal/trading name, address, working contact and applicable registration details
Itinerary Day-by-day plan, overnight stops and realistic travel expectations
Price Itemised total, inclusions, exclusions, taxes and payment schedule
Transport Vehicle category, passenger/luggage capacity and local-transfer conditions
Driver support Driver contact process and agency escalation contact
Hotels Exact property, room category, meal plan and substitution policy
Houseboat Property name, location, room/facility details and transfer arrangements
Activities Provider, ticket status, date, inclusions and cancellation terms
Reviews Recent, detailed feedback across independent sources
After-booking support Named contact, emergency process and written change policy

Questions to ask before paying an advance

  1. What is the exact name and address of the business taking my booking?
  2. Can you send the complete day-by-day itinerary and itemised quotation?
  3. Which hotels and houseboat will be booked, and what room categories are included?
  4. What vehicle category is confirmed, and are any local transfers separate?
  5. Which tickets and activities are confirmed, optional or payable directly?
  6. What costs are not included in the package price?
  7. What are the cancellation, refund and date-change conditions?
  8. Who will assist me after I arrive in Kashmir?

If the answers are vague, ask for clarification in writing before making payment.

Considering a locally based Kashmir travel agency

A local operator can be useful when you want one team to coordinate accommodation, private transport and destination planning from Srinagar. However, local presence alone does not prove quality; the same checks on registration, suppliers, written pricing, reviews and support still apply.

Final advice: choose clarity and dependable support over a “best agency” label

There is no single agency that is automatically best for every traveller. A couple planning a short honeymoon, a family travelling with young children and a group needing a large vehicle may need different services. The best fit is the agency that understands your requirements, gives you verifiable business details, names the suppliers, explains the full cost and provides a clear plan for support if something changes.

Take time to compare at least two or three written quotations. Confirm every important service before paying, keep your booking documents and make sure you know who to contact during the trip. These steps can help you choose a travel agency in Kashmir with greater confidence and make your holiday easier to manage.
